Privacy Policy & Terms

Last updated: November 2026

GharSure is a student project. This page explains, in plain words, what we collect and why. Have it reviewed by a lawyer before running GharSure as a business.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, phone number and a password (stored only as a salted hash, never in plain text).
  • Listings: flat details, photos and the map location you choose. Photos are re-saved without hidden data such as the GPS location your camera adds.
  • Bookings and bills: visit dates, messages, rent and bill amounts, and the UPI reference (UTR) you enter.
  • Roommate profile: lifestyle preferences you choose to share, such as sleep time, food habits and budget.
  • Scam reports: reported phone numbers are stored only as a one-way hash.

2. Why we use it

  • To show listings, connect renters with owners and flatmates, and keep a record of rent and bills.
  • To protect users from scams: photos, prices and messages are checked automatically by our AI.
  • We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

3. Who can see your phone number

Your number is hidden by default. It is shared only after both sides agree: when an owner or tenant accepts a visit request, or when two people accept each other as roommates. A tenant who is moving out may share their owner's number only if the owner agreed.

4. Payments

GharSure never receives or holds money. Rent and bills are paid directly from the tenant to the owner through UPI. We only store the amount and the reference number you enter, so both sides have a record.

5. Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data at any time. In India, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 gives you these rights. Contact the project owner through the GitHub page linked below.

6. Rules for using GharSure

  • Post only flats you own, or rooms and flats you actually live in.
  • Use real photos and honest prices. Listings our AI flags as high risk are hidden.
  • Never ask for money before a visit. Report anyone who does.
  • Our risk score is an automated estimate, not a guarantee. Always visit in person, check the owner's ID and property papers, and sign an agreement before paying anything.

7. If something goes wrong

If you've been scammed, call the cyber crime helpline 1930 and file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Then report the number on GharSure so others are warned.
