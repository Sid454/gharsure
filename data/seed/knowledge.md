## Should I pay token money before visiting the flat?
No. Never pay token, advance or deposit before you have visited the flat in person, met the owner, and checked the property documents. Genuine owners always let you see the flat first. Requests to pay before a visit are the most common rental scam.

## The owner says he is in the army and will courier the keys
This is a well-known scam script. The "army officer / CRPF / transferred suddenly" story is used to explain why the owner cannot meet you. Real owners or their family can show the flat. Do not pay and report the number.

## How do I verify that the person is the real owner?
Ask to see the electricity bill or property tax receipt in the owner's name and match it with their ID. Check the society office or neighbours. The rent agreement should be signed by the person whose name is on the ownership documents.

## What should a rent agreement contain?
Names and addresses of owner and tenant, property address, monthly rent, security deposit amount and refund terms, lock-in period, notice period, maintenance and electricity responsibility, rent increase clause, and signatures of both parties with two witnesses. Registering the agreement or using e-stamp paper protects both sides.

## Someone sent me a QR code or collect request to give me a refund
Scanning a QR code or approving a UPI collect request always sends money OUT of your account. You never need to scan or enter your UPI PIN to receive money. Decline it immediately.

## Somebody is asking for an OTP to confirm booking
Never share an OTP with anyone. OTPs authorise payments and logins. No genuine owner or broker needs your OTP.

## I have already been scammed, what should I do?
Call the national cyber crime helpline 1930 immediately and file a complaint at cybercrime.gov.in. Contact your bank to block or reverse the transaction. Keep screenshots of chats, payment receipts and the listing. Report the phone number on GharSure so others are warned.

## How much security deposit is normal?
In most Indian cities, 2 to 3 months of rent is common, while Bengaluru has traditionally asked for higher deposits. Many states now follow the Model Tenancy Act guideline of a maximum of 2 months for residential property. Always get a receipt and write the deposit amount in the agreement.

## Is it safe to pay a broker fee before seeing the flat?
No. Brokers normally charge only after the agreement is signed. A "registration fee" or "visit charge" before showing the property is a red flag.

## Police verification of tenants
Many cities require the owner to submit tenant verification to the local police. It is normally free and can be done online on the state police portal. Anyone asking you to pay "police verification charges" before a visit is suspicious.
